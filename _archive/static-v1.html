What we treat

From back pain to complex brain surgery, you get a clear diagnosis and a plan you understand.

1

Spine surgery

Slipped disc, narrow spinal canal, spinal tuberculosis, fractures and spinal tumors.

2

Endoscopic spine surgery

Smaller cuts, less pain and a faster return to daily life, where it is safe.

3

Brain tumors

Diagnosis and surgery for brain and skull base tumors.

4

Vascular neurosurgery

Brain aneurysms, bleeding in the brain and cavernomas.

5

Head and spine injury

Care after accidents and falls, with proper follow up.

6

Children's neurosurgery

Hydrocephalus and other brain and spine problems in children.
